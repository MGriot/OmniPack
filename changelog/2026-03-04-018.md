# Changelog - 2026-03-04

## [018] Accessibility Scoring & Stop ID Grouping
- Updated `core/models.py`:
    - Added `stop_id` to `Item` model to represent discharge sequence.
    - Implemented `calculate_accessibility` in `Container` to provide a "stop-aware" accessibility metric.
    - Added `stop_id` and `accessibility` to JSON serialization.
- Updated `core/engine_v2.py`:
    - Modified item sorting to prioritize `stop_id` (pack last stops first at the back).
    - Items for earlier stops are naturally pushed towards the door or placed in unblocked floor spots.
- Verified with `tests/test_accessibility.py`:
    - Confirmed that correct discharge sequencing results in 100% accessibility.
    - Confirmed that saturation correctly stacks items by stop.
    - Confirmed that blocked items (incorrect order) are penalized in the accessibility score.
- Updated tracking files (`tasks.json`, `stage.md`).
