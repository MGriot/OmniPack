# Changelog - 2026-03-01

## [011b] Real Physics - Stability & Force Transfer
- Implemented **Center of Gravity (CoG)** stability check in `core/accelerated.py`.
    - Items are now rejected if their geometric center is not physically supported by the items below (prevents "magic overhangs").
- Refined **Force Transfer Logic**:
    - Ensures **100% of an item's weight** is distributed to its supports.
    - Distribution is proportional to the **contact surface area**.
    - If a box is 50% on Box A and 50% overhang (but stable due to CoG on A), Box A takes 100% of the load.
- Updated `calculate_cumulative_loads` to use Z-descending sort for accurate pressure propagation from top to bottom.
- Verified physical rules within the Numba-accelerated engine.
