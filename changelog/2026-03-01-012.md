# Changelog - 2026-03-01

## [012] Intelligent Search - MCTS (Thinking Engine)
- Implemented **Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)** in `core/mcts.py`.
    - Mimics human "exploratory thinking" by simulating multiple future packing sequences before committing to a move.
    - Uses **UCT (Upper Confidence Bound for Trees)** to balance exploration of new spots and exploitation of known good ones.
    - Performs rapid "rollouts" using the Level 2 engine to predict global volume utilization.
- Integrated MCTS into `MultiContainerEngine` and the FastAPI `/pack` endpoint.
- Updated `viewer.html` with the **"Intelligent (MCTS)"** option.
- Refined `Level2Engine` scoring to strictly prioritize floor space (`Z, Y, X` order), reducing unnecessary vertical drift.
- Verified that MCTS finds better local minima in complex item sets compared to greedy BFD.
