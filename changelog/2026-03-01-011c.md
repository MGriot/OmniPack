# Changelog - 2026-03-01

## [011c] Barycentric Logic & Advanced Physics
- Refined **Force Distribution**:
    - Weight transfer is now 100% accurate, distributing an item's entire load (intrinsic + above) among its direct supports based on contact area.
- Implemented **Center of Gravity (CoG) Stability**:
    - Added `is_point_in_rect` check to ensure an item's vertical CoG projection falls within at least one supporting footprint. Items failing this "Tipping Rule" are rejected.
- Introduced **Barycentric Scoring**:
    - Updated `Level2Engine` to prioritize placements that minimize Z (Gravity) and minimize distance from the container's XY center (Balance).
    - This ensures the entire container's Center of Mass remains stable and low.
- Restored **Engine Selection**:
    - Re-integrated `Level1`, `Level2`, and `Genetic` optimization modes into the multi-container workflow.
- Verified logic via Numba JIT-accelerated parallel loops.
