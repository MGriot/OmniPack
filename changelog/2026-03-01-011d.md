# Changelog - 2026-03-01

## [011d] Floor Prioritization & Space Efficiency
- Refined the **Extreme Points Algorithm**:
    - Improved point hashing and comparison logic to maintain higher precision.
    - Simplified point generation to focus on valid boundary-aligned corners.
- Updated **Scoring System** in `Level2Engine`:
    - Changed from center-first (barycentric) scoring to **Bottom-Left-Back** priority.
    - New Priority: `(Z, Y, X)`. 
    - Effect: Items will now strictly fill the floor level and tuck into corners before attempting to stack, maximizing contiguous free space.
- Fixed the "Stacking Drift":
    - Stability and physics rules (CoG and weight) are still enforced, but they no longer "pull" items to stack in the center of the container if floor space is available.
- Verified improved floor utilization in multi-item scenarios.
